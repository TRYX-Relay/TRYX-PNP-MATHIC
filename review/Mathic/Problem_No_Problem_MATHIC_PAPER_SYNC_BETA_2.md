# 1. Lyric-reference architecture

The lyric layer has four jobs:

```text
1. HUMANIZE the score without changing it.
2. SIGNAL where conventional mathematics becomes active.
3. ROUTE the paper toward the correct outside source at first activation.
4. KEEP internal theorem authority separate from external context.
```

Pointer syntax:

```text
OUTSIDE POINTERS:
[R# : RELATIONSHIP_CLASS]
```

Relationship classes:

```text
FOUNDATION
HISTORICAL_ANTECEDENT
INDEPENDENT_SUPPORT
ANALOGOUS_METHOD
BOUNDARY_REFERENCE
REPRESENTATION_CAUTION
PROOF_BARRIER_CONTEXT
```

A lyric may also carry:

```text
INTERNAL POINTERS:
[SOURCE]
[LEAN]
[REPLAY]
[ORACLE]
```

when the statement being sung is actually established by the PNP package rather than by outside literature.

```text
REFERENCE_POINTER_IS_PROOF = NO
EXTERNAL_REFERENCE_IS_INTERNAL_AUTHORITY = NO
INTERNAL_RESULT_PRIMARY_AUTHORITY = INTERNAL_SOURCE / LEAN / REPLAY
```

# 3. Lyric P01 — Two questions

> **First ask whether the fold tells the truth. Then ask what it costs to carry the truth. Do not spend the same coin twice.**

### What the lyric is doing

It separates semantic correctness from conventional computational complexity.

```text
SEMANTIC_QUESTION = DOES_THE_FINITE_FOLD_PRESERVE_EXISTENTIAL_TRUTH
COMPLEXITY_QUESTION = IS_THE_TOTAL_STANDARD_MACHINE_COST_POLYNOMIAL_IN_ORIGINAL_INPUT_LENGTH
```

### Outside pointers

```text
[R4 : BOUNDARY_REFERENCE]
  Conventional P versus NP target and polynomial-time requirement.

[R5 : FOUNDATION / BOUNDARY_REFERENCE]
  Modern machine-complexity language and original-input cost discipline.

[R9 : BOUNDARY_REFERENCE]
  Reviewer-facing context for why semantic tractability and computational tractability are distinct.
```

### Internal pointers

```text

[REPLAY : finite semantic execution]
```

### Paper activation

Use R4/R5 when the paper first defines the conventional complexity obligation. R9 is optional for exposition and discussion.
